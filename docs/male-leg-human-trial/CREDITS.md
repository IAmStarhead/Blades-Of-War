## 100STYLE and HumanMotionCraft — new crouch and gait references

**100STYLE**, Ian Mason, Sebastian Starke and Taku Komura, <https://www.ianxmason.com/100style/> and <https://zenodo.org/records/8127870>, licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The source is motion capture of one actor performing deliberately styled motions, not an anatomical standard.

We use HumanMotionCraft's [Crouch walk 7](https://github.com/YalKouassi/HumanMotionCraft/blob/5eda21e0be7e2c9912b204eabb1a5d79ecd90d06/assets/library/animations/Locomotion/Crouch%20walk%207.clip.json) (SHA-256 `f44ef97de42d3c2a468d5d8a14156a0ede4deee42550bd9e5a0b70a8a805f184`) and [Walk 12](https://github.com/YalKouassi/HumanMotionCraft/blob/5eda21e0be7e2c9912b204eabb1a5d79ecd90d06/assets/library/animations/Locomotion/Walk%2012.clip.json) (SHA-256 `a4fd36340b55d45ed1e92c85783ace73bf43f2864f0839f61c5edec4ed3c7222`). HumanMotionCraft retargeted 100STYLE to its rig and cut three-second clips; see its [attribution](https://github.com/YalKouassi/HumanMotionCraft/blob/5eda21e0be7e2c9912b204eabb1a5d79ecd90d06/ATTRIBUTION.md) and [notice](https://github.com/YalKouassi/HumanMotionCraft/blob/5eda21e0be7e2c9912b204eabb1a5d79ecd90d06/NOTICE).

For the **new** trial we removed horizontal root movement and yaw, selected and crossfaded frames 42–78, converted coordinate axes, transferred the pelvis and leg directions to a different 52-bone rig, slowed 1.2 seconds of source to a 1.6-second loop, changed foot pitch in swing and baked a support-point IK fit at 0.38 m/s. Runtime blending, floor correction and knee-volume morphs add further changes. The stationary crouch is **constructed** by mirroring/averaging directions from frame 48; it is not a recorded idle. Arms come from the earlier trial. Intermediate keys use SLERP/linear rather than HumanMotionCraft's SQUAD/Hermite interpolation. The comparison video and `evidence/reference-*.jpg`, `evidence/compare-*.jpg` render these adapted or reference poses, cropped and assembled into sheets.

The HumanMotionCraft [default rig](https://github.com/YalKouassi/HumanMotionCraft/blob/5eda21e0be7e2c9912b204eabb1a5d79ecd90d06/assets/rig/default_rig.json) (SHA-256 `24979f497bc7b75eaea811cd078ddb6d1d054be305fa2c1712a3e14605f60d73`) and [mannequin](https://github.com/YalKouassi/HumanMotionCraft/blob/5eda21e0be7e2c9912b204eabb1a5d79ecd90d06/assets/rig/mannequin.glb) (SHA-256 `89c6af32ae2c743d7ffe57098aeb6d4e4aad6e9408782344daccb1aa07595b63`) used for reference renders derive from **Anny**, Copyright © 2025 NAVER Corporation, [Apache-2.0](LICENSE_ANNY_APACHE-2.0.txt), and MakeHuman MPFB2, CC0 1.0. HumanMotionCraft is independent of NAVER. The mannequin is rendered in the reference sheets; it is not embedded in the interactive scene. The original [HumanMotionCraft NOTICE](NOTICE_HUMANMOTIONCRAFT.txt) is retained in full; it also describes upstream components not used in this trial.

## CMU motion capture — independent references

The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

- The running reference is HumanMotionCraft's retargeted [Run 9](https://github.com/YalKouassi/HumanMotionCraft/blob/5eda21e0be7e2c9912b204eabb1a5d79ecd90d06/assets/library/animations/Locomotion/Run%209.clip.json) (dataset `cmu`, SHA-256 `d46f8eae1c8cc1eae338eba0d5b00168f1faf0553322d81dc5d6006df7fa745b`), rendered on its mannequin at selected exact keys. The original run remains a separate person's recording; our walk/run animations are not taken from this clip.
- The independent crouch reference `evidence/reference-cmu-crouch.jpg` is a line projection of [CMU 136_09](https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap/blob/09a07f54f3bbb58797325f009282d0b2048a2871/data/136/136_09.bvh), SHA-256 `f958ad4cfc516b025da94eb1085b7ded2f34e0fad2c0520fb554a29794b28c33`. It is not a textured model or video. BVH conversion by Bruce Hahne; his [READMEFIRST](CMU_BVH_READMEFIRST.txt) describes the conversion and quotes an earlier broad permission. The [current CMU homepage](https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/) also states the more specific condition that the data itself must not be resold directly, even in converted form; consult that source for its current terms.
