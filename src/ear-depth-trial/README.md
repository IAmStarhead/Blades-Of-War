# Ear root depth experiment — incomplete

Left is the previous `ear-detail-trial` result. Right keeps the ear-root insert's height and depth but makes it thinner along the outward axis and moves it slightly toward the skull. Head and body vertices, ear tilt and neck length are unchanged; earlier models remain untouched. Ear geometry comes from CC0 MakeHuman-derived assets as documented in `../head-reference/README.md`.

The prominent oval is less obvious, but the head still shows through the cut ear mesh, leaving a flat light patch and a dark discontinuity, especially on the male. This is not a rebuilt concha or a weld, nor is it a finished result. A separate test removing the insert and pushing the ear outward exposed cropped scalp edges and was discarded. Animation has not been checked. Comparison screenshots and enlarged crops are in `docs/previews/ear-depth-trial/`.

Browser checks at desktop and mobile exercised controls and checked identical head/body vertex positions, independent body textures, and finite nonempty ear geometry. These checks do not establish artistic quality or animation compatibility.
