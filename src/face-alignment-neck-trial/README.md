# Eye alignment, ears and neck — isolated trial

Open `/src/face-alignment-neck-trial/` via Vite. The left recreates the right-hand version from `feature-neck-skin-trial` with the bridge off; the right is the new trial. No original game assets are edited or replaced.

* Socket/brow contributions in `refineFace` and `headPlanes` are shifted by +.010 in vertical UV for both sexes and by -.055 radians towards the centre for the male, roughly following the previous texture repositioning. These are broad, small changes in existing relief, not reconstructed eyelids or verified anatomical alignment; other underlying facial relief is unchanged. Clay and alternate-light captures are included. The effect is subtle.
* Female existing eye/lid artwork is locally magnified by at most 1.07 with feathering, and the upper line is very slightly darkened using adjacent pixels; this preserves the existing drawing rather than drawing new eyes. Local feathering means the visible enlargement is not uniformly 7%. Brows are not deliberately enlarged. There is no separate eyelash geometry.
* Nose: symmetric subtle surface bumps, at most .0012 local units, around the sides of the existing tip. No actual nostril openings are cut and no claim is made that new anatomy is complete. Ears and their roots move forward along local Z by .0025 (male) and .0015 (female); this is an approximation, not an exact 5% of any anatomical measure. Ears remain separate meshes.
* Neck bridge button is **off by default**. On the right it tests a lower body ring than the prior skinned bridge, with interpolation of the ring's bone weights to the new bridge. In profile the previous big gap changes to a visible toothed edge/collar and shading discontinuity. Neck looks short and stiff. This attempt is unsuccessful; the meshes are not welded and the bridge should not be integrated. The bridge changes geometry and volume, while the standard (off) neck remains unchanged. Fixed yaw/pitch buttons provide pose snapshots, not an animation sequence or compatibility proof. The 20-segment body ring and 64-segment bridge remain separate.

`node tests/face-alignment-neck-trial.mjs` checks synthetic bridge weight normalization, head-bone rotation response and bounded finite nose geometry. `npm run verify`: 232/232. Screenshots cover both sexes, front/three-quarter/profile, clay and other light, plus neck in neutral, yaw and pitch. Neither automated checks nor screenshots certify artistic placement, mesh welding, full animation, hair or garments. Ears derive from existing CC0 trial described in `../head-reference/README.md`.
