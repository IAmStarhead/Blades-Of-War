# Expanded ear surface experiment

Separate comparison based on the accepted female-chin-forward study. The right side extracts a broader patch of the CC0 MakeHuman source geometry (see `../head-reference/README.md`); the previous ellipsoid insert stays hidden. The male ear also uses a more aggressive back-margin trim. A symmetric static vertex tint is applied to the underlying head near the ear bowl, not a real shadow. The prior trial's additional ear tint is not used. Left is the accepted baseline. On the right, the prior stitchConcha procedure is skipped because the expanded crop does not have the boundary loop expected by that algorithm; this also means its localized head recess is not applied on the right.

**Unfinished**: expanding the crop has not reconstructed a complete anatomical concha: exposed head still reads through the center, and the backing tint can spill as a smudge outside the ear depending on view/light. Male trim reduces one rear fragment but may remove useful triangles. Ear and head remain separate overlapping meshes, with no demonstrated watertight repair, and the neck seam is unchanged. Do not integrate. Face drawing and original assets are not replaced; side-of-head geometry differs locally because of the skipped stitch step. Turn/tilt are static views, not a full animation test.

Open `/src/ear-restored-surface-trial/` in Vite. Screenshots (including enlarged crops) are in `docs/previews/ear-restored-surface-trial/`. Run `node tests/ear-restored-surface-trial.mjs`; `npm run verify` passed 232/232. Tests don't validate anatomy or connectivity.
