# Gaze alignment trial

Side-by-side female/male comparison based on the medium-density B heads from `../head-balance/` (source and CC0 attribution in `../head-reference/README.md`). Both sides use identical lighting, neutral materials and temporary brow guides. The right pair repositions each iris and pupil to the centroid of the visible portion of its eyeball through the head mesh, as sampled from the front. The correction is intended for this frontal rest pose and is not an animated gaze rig or proof of alignment at every angle. It runs before the head group is moved or rotated. This is a visual experiment, not integrated into the game.

Run Vite and open `/src/head-gaze/`. Browser captures are in `docs/previews/head-gaze/`.
