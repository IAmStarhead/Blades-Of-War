# Character look trial — unfinished

Open `/src/character-look-trial/` on the Vite server. Independent copies only: the left figure is the existing face-orbit trial, the right adds experimental hair, and on the male, beard and moustache. No game assets or original characters are replaced.

The hair cap is sampled from the head UV surface and has its own geometry and generated texture. A simple bun distinguishes the female test. The beard and moustache are separate shallow meshes using the same generated hair texture. Skin grain is added to a cloned head map and an ear map based on the previously sampled skin colour; it does not weld the ear seam or guarantee matching lighting. The eyes, lips, nose and face relief remain from previous trials, not a new finished face.

This is not approved artwork: hair still reads like a cap, the bun is spherical, beard edges are angular, and the mouth opening and moustache need work. The moustache is separate but overlaps the beard only at its ends, not yet a convincing layered construction. The hair shapes are not distinct regional styles, are not tested with headwear or animation, and do not yet cast realistic shadows. Realistic lighting and skin/ear continuity remain unfinished.

References consulted: [Project Zomboid hair meshes and texture workflow](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2721464487), [hair silhouette and clumps](https://gamesartist.co.uk/curly-hair-ls/), [real-time hair design](https://yelzkizi.org/real-time-hair-for-aaa-games/), [stylized character head examples](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models/character/man/free-stylized-male-hairstyle). No assets copied from these references.
