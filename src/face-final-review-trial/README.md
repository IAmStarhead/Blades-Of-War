# Face and original-neck review, isolated

Open `/src/face-final-review-trial/` through Vite. Left reconstructs the right-hand, bridge-off side of `face-alignment-neck-trial`; right applies a further pass. Original game meshes and all previous studies are untouched. No hair work is included.

* The female eye and eyebrow artwork is warped about 4 canvas pixels inward per side, after the prior eye magnification. It is still the same drawn eyes, not separate eye meshes. Female lip drawing is locally expanded vertically up to 1.22 with a feathered falloff; a shallow projection is added to the mouth area. Its apparent size is a visual judgment, not a promise of anatomically modelled lips.
* Both faces receive an additional bilateral cheek, eye socket and brow displacement. A shallow curved male brow ridge approximately follows the drawn brow in UV space, rather than an independently sculpted orbital anatomy; exact registration to a curved textured surface and the final appearance require visual review. The source textures and ridge are not geometrically linked. Clay captures reveal the limited actual depth and can be judged independently of the drawing.
* The rejected skinned bridge is NOT used. Both sides retain the previous separate head and body geometry. On the right only, near-intersection normals are blended between the two rest-pose surfaces with a small radius and weight. This does not connect meshes, change head/neck volume or repair a gap, silhouette, material boundary, or animated seam. The seam remains plainly visible in profile; this is **not a completed neck repair**. Fixed yaw and pitch pose captures are not a full animation test; rest-pose-matched normals may be inappropriate when moving.

`node tests/face-final-review-trial.mjs` checks bounded, symmetric relief on a synthetic head, finite normals and that neck normal matching changes no positions. `npm run verify` 232/232. Browser screenshots cover front, three-quarter, both profiles, clay, opposite light, two fixed poses and body scale for both figures. Tests do not judge appearance, accurately prove brow/relief alignment, or validate animation. The prior ear provenance is documented in `../head-reference/README.md`.
