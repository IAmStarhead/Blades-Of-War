# Larger eyes, lips and relief — experimental

Both sides use the softened `face-form-study` base with identical twice-relaxed ears. Left shows the original painted eyes and mouth on this base, not the right-hand eye redraw of `face-expression-trial`. Right has larger painted eyes centred three texture pixels inward, a new upper/lower lip drawing expanded vertically around its prior centre, and a modest additional broad cheek/orbit/brow relief. Nose and mouth geometry are protected; lip projection is unchanged and the lips are not volumetric. Earlier models and game assets remain untouched.

The lips are an artist's stylised interpretation, informed by anatomical landmarks: upper lip has a shallow Cupid's bow, lower lip a broader curve, with tapered corners and philtrum above (https://blog.3d.sk/2025/04/23/exploring-mouth-anatomy-for-artists-with-logan-wiesen/ and https://syraaesthetics.com/blog/cupids-bow-everything-you-need-to-know/). This is not a claim of perfect anatomy.

The female eyes may look surprised and lips rather painted, the male mouth may no longer read as the earlier gentle smile, and new relief remains subtle at normal scale. Face/ear seams and faceting persist; no new ear modifications, eye animation or nation-specific faces in this trial. Ear asset provenance: CC0 MakeHuman-derived (`../head-reference/README.md`).

Desktop and mobile browser checks exercised views, both lights and distances; verified body, head height and protected nose/lip coordinates, ear indices and texture isolation. Changes to texture confined to eye and mouth regions; nose texture centre unchanged. `npm run verify` passed 232 checks. These do not establish artistic quality or animation compatibility. PNGs under `docs/previews/face-features-trial/`.
