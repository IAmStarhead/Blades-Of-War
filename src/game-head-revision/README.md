# Game character head comparison — visual trial

Isolated comparison only: original `bowCharacter` bodies, skeleton, clothing and head remain unchanged. The trial hides the original head on a copy and attaches a pre-existing CC0 MakeHuman-derived `../head-balance/` medium-detail head to its head bone. See `../head-reference/README.md` for source/license and `../head-balance/README.md` for details. This is not a new sculpt: the head has been resized and the cut neck tapered to overlap the game's existing neck. The male head is widened slightly to avoid reducing the old head's bounding width. Eyes/brows are temporary guides and hair/beard are hidden on both figures.

The join is an overlap, not welded or skinned together: a color/shading discontinuity and shape mismatch are visible at the neck, especially in profile. The higher-detail head can clash with the original body style, and facial expressions, hairstyles, beard, poses and animation have not been validated. Not integrated into gameplay or editor. This is a visual fit test, not a production head.
