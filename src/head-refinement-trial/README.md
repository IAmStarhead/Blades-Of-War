# Isolated head refinement experiment

Comparison on copies of the original figures: left is the right-hand version of `female-profile-trial`; right makes the female upper cranium lower/narrower, slightly narrows the lower jaw, and draws fuller upper/lower lips on a cloned texture. Both necks receive a small width-only taper on copied body vertices, with no deliberate change in length. Male face geometry and mouth are kept as in the previous trial. On the right, colour at the bottom of a cloned head texture is blended towards body skin in an attempt to reduce contrast; it does not connect the meshes or remove their geometric seam. The line remains visible. Lips remain painted, not separate anatomical geometry.

The underlying original UV-mapped head uses a smoothed lower-face displacement sampled from a CC0 MakeHuman-derived reference, not an attached replacement head; see `../head-reference/README.md` for provenance. No original game models, previous trials or editor presets are replaced. This is a static visual trial only, with hair and ears hidden; neck animation and deformation have not been validated. Open `/src/head-refinement-trial/` with Vite; PNGs are in `docs/previews/head-refinement-trial/`.
