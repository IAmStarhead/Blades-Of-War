# Ear shape trial on copied game heads

A static visual comparison based on `straight-nose-trial` (central nose), with left earless and right a pair of hand-built simplified ear shells. Each ear has an asymmetric outline, narrowed lower lobe, a shallow bowl and an inner-fold suggestion. The two are mirrored, oriented outward and attached to the head bone; their front edges overlap the head. Both profiles, front and three-quarter views are available. The previously accepted nose and pre-bridge neck are retained. Consulted the user-supplied anatomical head/ear reference, including `i (7).webp` from origin/main.

Important limitation: these are separate overlapping meshes, NOT welded into the head, so they can still read as stuck-on and their simplified interior is not an accurate anatomical reconstruction. The fixed skin colour may not match all lighting or appearances. The ear test verifies symmetry, finite geometry, and following a parent transform, not full animated gameplay or artistic quality. No game/editor integration; old characters and earlier heads are unchanged. Hair/beard remain hidden and the older head-neck seam remains visible. The inherited jaw reference is CC0; see `../head-reference/README.md`.
