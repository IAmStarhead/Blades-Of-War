# Upper ear strip winding experiment

Both comparison columns recreate `ear-old-front-new-profile-trial` (63cb14d), including its old-front contour blend. Right reverses the winding of each newly appended upper-root strip triangle, making its shared boundary direction opposite to the ear triangle edge used to build it. Left retains the old strip winding. Original ear triangles, vertex positions, interpolated vertex normals, colours, lobe and head are the same; only strip index order changes. Materials are double-sided, so back-facing fragments reverse normals in shading and winding can change their apparent colour. This does not prove the entire cropped ear mesh is consistently oriented or watertight.

In the close-up three-quarter captures the large upper dark circular spot substantially shrinks for both sexes, with a narrow remaining mark. In front there are still uneven rims and a light spot on the man's upper ear, whose appearance may change with light. Ear and head remain separate overlapping meshes; this is not a weld and does not establish defect-free geometry. The small inner canal shading is unchanged. Full animation cycle untested; game models remain unchanged.

See `docs/previews/ear-root-winding-repair-trial/` including front and close-up images.
