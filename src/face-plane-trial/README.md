# Diagnostic: same head with and without facial texture

Both figures have identical head positions/normals and the same ear and body construction, under the same lights. Left has the calm face texture, right has no head texture (white material); the `Обе без рисунка` button makes both textureless, `Сравнить` restores the comparison. The head texture is not edited or destroyed, and original game models and earlier trials remain untouched. Ear geometry is CC0 MakeHuman-derived (`../head-reference/README.md`). This scene is diagnostic, not a new face.

The textureless view retains visible dark shading at the eye/brow region and beside the nose, demonstrating contributions from head surface/normals and lighting even in the absence of painted shadows. Its white base differs in colour from the skin texture, so this is a qualitative diagnosis, not a measurement of each contribution. The original texture also contains painted gradients and eye/brow marks. The current head was constructed as radial rings rather than facial loops, and eye/lip details are mostly painted; a round deformation that is large enough to read creates isolated cheek bulges or creases instead of a connected anatomical surface. Smooth shading/normals, earlier transferred forms and protected nose/mouth boundaries complicate the result. This is a limitation of the current construction and approaches, not proof that retopology alone will guarantee quality.

A diagonal cheek/under-eye experiment was inspected in the same session but discarded: it still produced blotchy transitions and was not a convincing face. No ear polishing, revised eyes, lips or new nation variants in this diagnostic. Head and ears remain separate. The next modeling approach should work on a separate copy and use anatomically organised local surface flow/transitions; preserve the original models and previously accepted nose, mouth and jaw and check seams and animation.

Desktop/mobile browser tests exercised controls, views, lights and distance, confirmed equal head positions, protected nose/lip geometry and body positions and ear indices. `npm run verify` passed 232 checks. Screenshots are in `docs/previews/face-plane-trial/`. These checks are not artistic or animation approval.
