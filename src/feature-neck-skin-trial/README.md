# Face placement and experimental skinned neck bridge

Isolated copies of the existing game figures; open `/src/feature-neck-skin-trial/` via Vite. The left reconstructs `eyes-raised-trial`'s right side. The right moves both male eye/brow drawings toward the nose by about 5.5 pixels relative to the left on the 512px texture. This is an inverse UV warp, not a change to the geometry, eye orientation or orbital anatomy; the apparent slant is not independently corrected. The female face drawing is unchanged. Both right-side ears are raised by .008 local units; the female ear patch is also inclined approximately 0.12 radians around its local horizontal axis, with the existing root raised to match. The ears remain separate, clipped meshes; placement and attachments need visual review. No head or neck vertex positions are changed in the normal (bridge-off) comparison.

The **Проба соединения** button switches only the right copy to an experimental skinned bridge. It reuses the previously rejected neck-transition geometry on a clone: trims body top triangles and lower head, inserts a bridge with 64 segments to the 20-segment body ring. The bridge/head is a new skinned mesh bound to the body's existing skeleton; its upper head is weighted to HEAD, and the lower boundary interpolates the body ring's HEAD/NECK skin weights. The original right head and body are retained and restored by **Шея прежняя**. This is *not* a single welded manifold: the bridge and body have separate vertices/materials and mismatched tessellation. The bridge changes the surface and enclosed volume and can create a collar/gap, unlike the non-bridge trial. In profile a visible step/gap remains; it is not a successful solution and must not be integrated.

Buttons put both models into a fixed 0.45 radian head yaw or -0.25 radian head pitch to inspect deformation, not an animation sequence. The skinned trial moves with the head bone and screenshots show those sample poses, but this is not a full animation, stress, garment, hair, or topology validation; the ears remain rigidly attached to the head bone. It cannot establish production safety. Female/male screenshots show face comparisons and bridge rest, turn and tilt, from front and side. Initial bridge state is off.

`node tests/feature-neck-skin-trial.mjs` validates isolated synthetic geometry, normalized weights and rotation response; browser checks on the two actual figures found unchanged non-bridge head positions, finite normalized bridge weights and movement under a head rotation. `npm run verify`: 232/232. These checks do not judge anatomy, weld quality, rendering across all angles or animation. Ears use the existing CC0 reference-derived trial (see `../head-reference/README.md`); no original game files were modified.
