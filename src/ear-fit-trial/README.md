# Ear fitting and colour experiment

Follow-up to `ear-reference-trial` on copies of the original game figures. Left is the previous extracted ear, right retains its anatomical shape, filters detached triangle components, uses a head-material clone with a skin-like colour selected separately for male/female, and adds a small buried overlapping root. This is a visual fit experiment, not a welded connection. Face/nose and the pre-bridge neck are inherited from the previous trial.

The ears are extracted from the CC0 MakeHuman-derived head study; see `../head-reference/README.md` for provenance and license. They are separate meshes attached to the head bone; the root is another intersecting mesh. Fixed colours are only approximations to the textured skin and can vary in appearance with lighting, skin settings and surface normals. Cropped edges, small triangular artifacts and partial separation can still be visible, particularly from the front. Do not interpret the test as a solved seamless/animated ear. No game/editor integration, or changes to original and older models. Hair/beard are hidden in the trial.

Vite URL `/src/ear-fit-trial/`, screenshots `docs/previews/ear-fit-trial/`. UI and geometry were tested in the browser; full animation and artistic quality were not verified.
