# New head study — NOT production-ready

Open `/src/new-head-study/` in Vite. Left is the previous head, right a new generated mesh on a copy of the old body; game assets and earlier trials remain untouched. The new mesh is generated from hand-authored cross-sections, a separate UV map, and simple mathematical nose, cheek and orbital shaping; it does not use the old head's vertices, MakeHuman head or automatic decimation. Its male/female shapes currently share the same cross-section template with different scale and slight feature coefficients; they are not independently sculpted anatomies. It remains a ring-based mesh, not a finished artist-authored face topology. Eyes, brows, mouth and nostrils are temporary 2D marks. The existing reference-derived ears are separate meshes shifted outward to fit; they are not welded and their colour, shape and attachment are not finished. The new nose and jaw are not copies of the previously approved shapes.

The first attempt had horizontal bands due to interpolation; monotone cubic interpolation reduced them. Ears were initially buried and were repositioned. Subsequent adjustments changed lower skull, eyes and mouth. It still looks too generic: visible dark nose/cheek shadows, weak facial features, short-looking neck junction and unresolved ear seams; it has not achieved convincing anatomical realism and should not replace the original. No hair, shadow-map, animation, rigging or production-performance validation.

Visual references consulted (not copied as assets): [front/profile anatomy and ear proportions](https://lifedrawing.academy/drawing-lessons/head-and-face-proportions), [front and profile proportions](https://jeffkamangara.wordpress.com/2018/12/10/the-head-in-perspective/), [female planar head](https://cgtrader.com/3d-models/character/anatomy/planes-of-the-head-female), [Asaro low-poly planar study](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/asaro-planes-of-the-head-low-poly-053ecc233af5466293e10ed19161c9de), and front/three-quarter/profile visual references. Ears are derived from CC0 MakeHuman assets; see `src/head-reference/README.md`.

Checks: `node tests/new-head-study.mjs` checks topology bounds, finite data and symmetry; `npm run verify` 232/232. Browser screenshots show both sexes, front, profile and three-quarter, opposite light, clay and mobile. Checks do not certify anatomy, stitching, deformation or artistic quality.
