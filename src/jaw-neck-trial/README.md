# Neck and female jaw — comparison trial

An isolated follow-up to `../jaw-reference-trial/`, shown side by side: the previous trial on the left, new variation on the right. Each uses copies of the original game characters; original game meshes and earlier trials remain untouched. Both show heads without ears, hair or beards.

The new variation reduces the earlier deformation of the lower rear skull, modifies the top back of the cloned neck, reduces the female jaw/chin width and lip projection, and makes the female mouth texture patch smaller. The male face front is intentionally kept close to the previous trial. This is a deformation of the original procedural head with the smoothed lower-face displacement sampled from the CC0 MakeHuman-derived `../head-balance/` mesh, not a new sculpt. See `../head-reference/README.md` for asset provenance.

The head and neck are still separate intersecting meshes, not welded. The visible material/shading boundary and the rear neck contour are not resolved; female profile remains in need of review. No integration or animation compatibility is claimed. Open `/src/jaw-neck-trial/` in Vite. Screenshots: `docs/previews/jaw-neck-trial/`.
