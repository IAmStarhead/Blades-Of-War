# Head planes study — separate, unfinished

Open `/src/head-plane-trial/` with Vite. Left: previous face form, right: head geometry test and the prior trial's smoothed ears. Original characters, previous trials and game files remain unchanged. Both bodies/neck copies match. Only the head is untextured in the “Без рисунка” diagnostic; ears and body retain their materials, so the differing colours in that mode are intentional.

The right head uses broad symmetric cheek, brow and socket displacements, a small flattening of the upper side of the skull and a small reduction of its back depth. The nose and lips are masked, the jaw height and body are retained. The first back-skull taper looked pointed, so its transition was broadened; stronger facial relief produced dark grooves and was reduced. The remaining relief is still subtle and the comparison does not establish anatomical correctness. The painted eyes and eyebrows were NOT relocated; they should be fitted after a satisfactory relief is chosen, not represented as finished. Ears retain the previous trial's higher triangle-count subdivision on the right; they are not welded, and attachment/size remain unresolved. No animation or hair compatibility test.

Checks: `node tests/head-plane-trial.mjs`; browser control checks in both sexes and four angles, two lights and two distances, plus desktop/mobile screenshots; protected points and identical body arrays were checked in browser. North sample max vertex movement 0.00542 female / 0.00630 male (mostly rear skull). `npm run verify` 232/232. Such tests cannot judge artistic quality or guarantee ear attachment.
