# Ear front surface trial

Both columns retain the upper-edge rounding of `ear-front-rounding-trial`. The right column applies a static localized shader tint to the rear ear surface exposed through the front-view opening (both sexes). The rear-facing surface was identified by ray probing; the shader darkens it smoothly to reduce the pale strip, without changing triangles or cutting a hole. Female right-side comparison additionally pulls the upper inner root slightly inward (up to .003 in local model units), with a tapered effect and differential normal update; the lower lobe and indices remain unchanged. Male ear shape and both faces remain unchanged from baseline.

Front PNGs show a less bright strip, not a genuine surface repair; angular contours and upper gaps remain, especially on the female. The tint is static, not a cast shadow, and could look different under other lighting. The ear and head overlap and are not welded. Profile/three-quarter, alternative lighting and static turn/tilt screenshots are included for checking. Full animation remains untested. No original game models replaced. The trial relies on Three.js shader chunks and needs explicit rendering and visual validation if integrated.

Open `/src/ear-front-surface-trial/` in Vite; images in `docs/previews/ear-front-surface-trial/`. `node tests/ear-front-surface-trial.mjs` checks synthetic geometry, lobe and index preservation and shader injection; screenshot capture exercised both characters and controls without page errors. These checks do not prove anatomical or animation quality. CC0 source documented at `../head-reference/README.md`.
