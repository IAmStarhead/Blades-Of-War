# Orbital alignment and neutral mouth; experimental neck union

Open `/src/orbital-neutral-trial/` via Vite. Left reconstructs the male right-hand result from `face-planes-rework-trial`, i.e. before the additional upward eye-art shift in `male-eye-placement-trial`. Right keeps that eye art position and attempts to remove the game head's old socket/lid displacement and replace it with a recess closer to the eyes and below the brow, in addition to the preceding cheek/brow rework. Removal is approximate because these procedural layers are embedded in geometry altered by earlier steps; shadows and alignment remain a visual judgment, not an anatomical guarantee. The right male mouth is redrawn in a level neutral line slightly higher; the shape, placement and expression need visual approval. Female copies are unchanged. No emotion switching is implemented.

Both columns default to the previous separate head and neck. **Эксперимент стыка (мужчина)** builds a separate skinned boolean-union trial on the right only: it computes a CSG union of cloned head and body surfaces, then transfers approximate weights from the nearest source surface/triangle to a single `SkinnedMesh`. Head and neck bone poses can be sampled with the buttons. This does not constitute a hand-built clean, welded animation topology: the intersection has artifacts and cracks, the body/garment relationship is damaged, and weight transitions are approximate. In the captured male example it generates ~69,098 triangles; some defects remain at the neckline and shoulder and deformation under head turns is not production-validated. It is unsuccessful and not safe to integrate. CSG itself changes geometry and may change volume; this is not the preserved-volume original. **Прежняя шея** restores the separate original copies. The experiment uses MIT-licensed `three-bvh-csg` and `three-mesh-bvh` development dependencies, not integrated into gameplay.

Original game assets and earlier trials remain unchanged. Screenshots cover male face views, clay, alternative light and fixed poses, plus the experimental union in rest/yaw/pitch. Head/neck pose snapshots are not a complete animation test; no hair or clothes animation was tested. Ear reference provenance: `../head-reference/README.md`.

`node tests/orbital-neutral-trial.mjs` checks synthetic orbital geometry differences and normalized, finite bone weights on a synthetic union; browser inspection of the actual male union measured finite normalized weights and valid indices, with both head and neck influences. Browser comparison of the non-union copies found male orbital positions differ up to ~.00412 local units, body positions identical, male texture changes confined to mouth rows, and female head geometry and texture identical. These checks do not prove watertightness, smooth deformation or visual correctness. `npm run verify`: 232/232.
