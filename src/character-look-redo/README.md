# Character look redo — isolated, unfinished

Run Vite at `/src/character-look-redo/`. Left: existing bald face-orbit baseline; right: independent experimental hair and beard. Previous trial and production characters remain untouched.

Changes from character-look-trial: added narrow swept lock meshes over the cap, reduced texture noise, replaced round female bun with a smaller rolled knot, changed beard silhouette and raised the separate moustache over its edges. An attempted rectangular sideburn looked disconnected and was removed. Hair and beard are separate from skin and use their own generated texture. Skin and ears keep the previous trial's subtle grain.

Limitations: locks still look cut into a cap rather than flowing hair, knot reads small and simple, beard's cheeks and exposed mouth remain unnatural and beard does not connect to the hair. Moustache overlays at its ends but needs better contour and layering. Neither hairstyles nor beards are ready for use or checked with animation/headwear. This trial does not deliver new face sculpt or realistic cast shadows. Original nose, jaw and facial expression are not replaced by a new face.

References: [Project Zomboid independent hair mesh/texture](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2721464487), [hair silhouette and clumps](https://gamesartist.co.uk/curly-hair-ls/). No reference assets copied.
