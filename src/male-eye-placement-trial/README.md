# Male eye and eyebrow placement, isolated comparison

Open `/src/male-eye-placement-trial/` via Vite. Left and right reconstruct the right-hand version of `face-planes-rework-trial`; the right additionally warps the male eye and eyebrow texture upward by 6 pixels in the central area of a 512×512 canvas, with feathered falloff. This moves the existing eye, brow and nearby painted shading together without deliberately redrawing, resizing or changing horizontal spacing. Distortion can occur in the feathered edges, so exact shape and eye-to-brow spacing are a visual question. Nose, mouth, cheek geometry, ears, head/neck positions and normals are unchanged between columns. The female comparison is unchanged on both sides. No game assets or previous studies are replaced.

This moves artwork over the existing surface, not actual eyes or orbit geometry. Alignment is approximate and must be judged under different lights and viewpoints. Lighting may still produce shading above the eyebrow. The head and neck are still separate and their seam remains. Fixed pose buttons are only visual pose samples, not an animation validation. Ear-source provenance is documented in `../head-reference/README.md`.

Validation: `node tests/male-eye-placement-trial.mjs` checks that synthetic eye and brow marks move up in tandem and distant mouth pixels are unchanged. Browser comparison of both actual trial figures verified head/body positions, normals and UV attributes are identical between columns, that male pixels changed only above row 300, and the female texture was unchanged. Screenshots show both angles and alternate light/clay. `npm run verify`: 232/232. None of these tests establish artistic quality or accurate anatomical registration.
