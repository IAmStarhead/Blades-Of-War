# Northern and central nose shape visual trial

The same north-based game-figure copies are shown on both sides to compare only nose shapes, not complete kingdom characters or new presets. The left adds a little width around the male nasal wings and softens the female ridge; the right reduces projection and subtly narrows the tip for a smaller straighter candidate. Differences are small and the central side may still read rounded in profile; neither is an approved final design. The inherited nasal root, lips, jaw, existing neck and other body proportions are retained. Nostril marks are painted texture, not modeled cavities.

This study subdivides copies of the existing game head, derived via earlier trial shaping with a CC0 reference jaw contour (see `../head-reference/README.md`). It does not replace old heads, characters, editor presets or the real kingdom-specific models. Hair and ears are hidden: no acceptable modeled ears are provided. The visible neck seam and animation remain unresolved.

Run Vite at `/src/nose-kingdom-trial/`; PNGs are in `docs/previews/nose-kingdom-trial/`. `node tests/nose-kingdom-trial.mjs` checks isolated/local finite geometry, not visual quality.
