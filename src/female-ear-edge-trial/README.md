# Female ear edge fitting experiment

The left comparison is the female `female-ear-fit-trial`; on the right, the low outer portion of each extracted ear is gently moved into the copied head surface using sideward ray intersections. This attempts to hide the cut-off scalp fragments at the base without removing the raised ear. The two sides fit the head separately and may differ slightly. The male comparison retains the same `ear-join-trial` ears on both sides, and head/nose/body are unchanged.

This is still an overlapping mesh, not a topological weld: shading, edge lines, some cropped detail and fit can remain visible, especially from other angles. The ear colour is approximate, the result has not been tested in animation, and visual quality needs approval. CC0 ear geometry attribution appears in `../head-reference/README.md`. All changes are limited to copies; original game characters and past trials are untouched.

Vite URL `/src/female-ear-edge-trial/`, PNGs `docs/previews/female-ear-edge-trial/`. Browser checks verify finite positions, male geometry unchanged between copies, approximate bilateral correspondence and view controls at desktop/mobile sizes, not artistic quality or animated behavior.
